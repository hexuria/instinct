# Dependency direction for hexuria/pua (spec §3.1).
#
# Format:
#   <crate>: <allowed pua-* deps, space separated>
#       | <banned crates anywhere in the crate's dependency closure; `*` is a glob>
#
# Checked by scripts/check_architecture.py in CI:
#   1. every workspace member has an entry (new crates must declare their direction);
#   2. a crate's normal and build dependencies on workspace crates are a subset of "allowed";
#   3. no banned crate appears anywhere in its resolved dependency closure (incl. dev-deps).
# `pua-pack-*` is a pattern entry that applies to every pack crate.
